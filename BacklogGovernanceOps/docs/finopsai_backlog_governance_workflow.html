Backlog Governance
Product Owner Workflow

Product Owner governed bridge from research completion to sprint intake.

This workflow begins when an approved research draft artifact is deposited to the intake folder and ends only when the resulting published PBI is either explicitly stopped or formally selected into Sprint Kanban. It governs intake, review, publication, stewardship, and sprint-readiness under Product Owner accountability.

Workflow Scope
Product Owner Governance Bridge

Use this workflow after research has produced approved markdown draft artifacts and before Product Development Step 1. It governs the Product Owner governance bridge from intake, review, and publication through backlog stewardship and sprint-readiness confirmation.

Product Owner Accountability

The Product Owner remains accountable for backlog administration throughout the workflow. LLM assistance and future MCP-assisted publication may support refinement or loading, but they do not replace explicit Product Owner review, approval, publication, or governance logging.

Visual Language

Neutral pills and badges describe process facts. Amber marks Product Owner control checks or gate conditions. Green marks confirmed outcomes or achieved workflow states.

Header Pill Logic

The header pills stay neutral by design because they describe stable workflow facts, not gate passage, exceptions, or completed outcomes.

Workflow Usage

How To Use This Workflow

Use this workflow when research has already ended with approved markdown draft artifacts and the Product Owner needs to govern what happens next. Start here when a draft enters the canonical intake path and continue here until the resulting PBI is either stopped, deferred, or confirmed as sprint-eligible and later sprint-selected.

See Workflow Usage Details
  • Workflow Usage Guide: Use this workflow to run the Product Owner bridge from intake through review, approval, publication, governance logging, backlog stewardship, and sprint-readiness confirmation. Keep research work in the research workflow, keep product delivery execution in Product Development, and use this bridge only for governed backlog administration between those two boundaries.
  • When to use: Use it when one or more approved research draft artifacts have arrived through the required file-system pattern /output/<name>_YYYY_MM_DD/<name>_clickup_card_draft_YYYY_MM_DD_<task-card-identifier>.md, when a part-time Product Owner needs a lean review queue, or when a published PBI still requires stewardship before Sprint Kanban selection.
  • Boundary reminder: Research ends with draft artifacts on disk, not published PBIs. Publication creates the real ClickUp PBI for the first time. Product Development begins only after the PBI is already published and sprint-eligible.
Product Owner Governance Bridge Map
  • Start: Approved draft artifact arrives in the canonical intake path.
  • Middle: Product Owner admits, reviews, approves, publishes, and records one approved draft artifact to one published PBI.
  • End: The PBI is either explicitly stopped, deferred under governance, or carried forward until sprint-ready and later sprint-selected.

Scope And Usage Map
What Is In Scope
  • Folder-based or future API-based draft intake into one canonical intake point.
  • Product Owner review, hold, admit, reject, approve, publish, and record actions.
  • Post-publication backlog stewardship through Sprint-Eligible and Sprint-Selected status.
What Is Out Of Scope
  • Transcript cleanup, source analysis, roadmap-fit assignment, or draft generation mechanics.
  • Sprint commitment, velocity planning, capacity negotiation, or delivery scheduling.
  • Automatic publication merely because a draft artifact exists on disk.
Lean Governance Constraint

The Product Owner is part-time. Each gate must produce a yes/no decision, an explicit hold, or a named output. The workflow stays formal without turning into a heavy review ceremony.

Inputs, Outputs, And Ownership Map
Inputs
  • Approved research markdown draft artifacts in the required file path pattern.
  • Run identifier, artifact count, task-card identifier, and source run context.
  • Existing ClickUp backlog context for duplicate and destination checks.
Outputs
  • Governed state transition from intake through Sprint-Selected or explicit stop.
  • Real ClickUp PBI in Backlog-Active, later Sprint-Eligible, then optionally Sprint-Selected.
  • Canonical governance log entries that reconnect the live PBI to the original draft artifact.
Roles And Ownership
  • Product Owner: review, admission, approval, publication, stewardship, sprint-readiness confirmation.
  • Scrum Master: unblock or escalate only when threshold rules are exceeded.
  • SME / End Users / Scrum Team / LLM: advisory support only; governance accountability remains with the Product Owner.

Shared Canonical State Rule

The intake folder is the canonical reopening point for newly deposited draft artifacts because both human and future agentic handling can verify the same artifact inventory, timestamps, and status transitions there.

Escalation Threshold Rule

If any deposited artifact remains unreviewed beyond the configured sprint-cycle threshold, Scrum Master notification is triggered. The default threshold is one sprint cycle, with an optional secondary count ceiling.

Notification Rule

The Product Owner is notified once per research run close with run ID and artifact count. Governance work then proceeds artifact by artifact inside that batch.

Secondary operating notes

The escalation threshold is configurable, but the default should stay simple enough to operate without continuous monitoring overhead. Count-based ceilings are a secondary signal for inbox accumulation, not a replacement for the time-based sprint-cycle threshold.

Recommended Handoff Contract

Use an event-backed folder handoff. The operational inbox remains the intake folder, duplicates are quarantined separately, and auditability plus dashboard visibility come from distinct record surfaces: a run-level receipt, a duplicate log, and later per-artifact governance decisions.

Need Recommended mechanism Why it fits
Simplify handoff Keep `01_intake` as the only shared inbox. No second active workflow surface to reconcile.
Handle duplicates safely Route duplicates to `01_intake_duplicates` and log them separately. Prevents overwrite while keeping the exception visible to audit and dashboard views.
Acknowledge delivery Auto-generate one receipt per research run. Provides proof of deposit without claiming PO action.
Acknowledge governance action Log explicit hold, admit, reject, approve, and publish decisions. Keeps business acknowledgment separate from system acknowledgment.
Support dashboard visibility Derive status from folder state plus receipts plus governance log. Dashboard reflects actual workflow evidence instead of inferred state.
Best-Fit Recommendation

Stay folder-based in Phase 1, but formalize the handoff as a run event triggered by run-summary approval, with one automatic delivery receipt, duplicate quarantine, and later explicit Product Owner decisions per artifact.

Dashboard Rule

The dashboard should show `Awaiting intake decision` when a receipt exists but no Product Owner intake decision has been logged yet.

Canonical Reopening Point

Reopen governance status from the governance log first, not from scattered folder names, isolated ClickUp status fields, or memory. The log reconnects the live PBI, the source draft artifact, and the decision history in one place.

Completion And Stop Conditions
Completion Definition
  • The draft artifact became one published PBI.
  • The PBI was stewarded to Sprint-Eligible and later selected into Sprint Kanban.
  • The governance log contains publication, stewardship, and sprint-selection history.
Defer Conditions
  • The item remains held in Intake with an explicit note.
  • The item remains staged in Review or Approved pending later publication timing.
  • The PBI remains Backlog-Active while stewardship continues.
Stop Conditions
  • The candidate is explicitly rejected with a reason note.
  • Publication cannot proceed because readiness gate or duplicate rule fails and the item is closed.
  • Sprint selection happens downstream and the governance close is recorded at Sprint-Selected.
Supporting note on terminal handling

Sprint-Selected is terminal for this governance workflow because Product Development owns the item from that point onward. If the item is later removed from sprint or reworked, reopen governance only through an explicit new decision rather than assuming the bridge is still active.

1Step 1: Receive And Acknowledge Draft Artifact

Research run closure triggers one Product Owner notification for the run ID and artifact count. Draft artifact(s) are deposited to the designated ClickUp intake folder, the required file path pattern is confirmed, receipt is written back to the research workspace run log, and the escalation timer starts per artifact from deposit timestamp.

Run-level notification Per-artifact timer Receipt written back
Procedural steps
  1. Open the governance workspace and confirm that new draft artifacts are expected in governance-states/01_intake/.
  2. Run the Step 1 Codex dry pass.
  3. Review the dry-run output and confirm the reported intake batch matches what you expect to see.
  4. Check whether any anomalies were reported. If anomalies exist, resolve them before continuing to Step 2.
  5. Confirm that the artifact filenames, receipt matches, and deposit timestamps look correct for this intake batch.
  6. If the dry-run result is correct, continue to Step 2 and persist the intake-triage acknowledgment.
Run in Codex

Use Codex to validate the intake inbox in dry-run mode before recording any triage acknowledgment.

Prompt to enter in Codex

Report: 1. Count of new artifacts 2. Artifact status table 3. PO review queue summary 4. Any anomalies 5. Next Step for the Product Owner

python3 scripts/run_intake_triage.py
Required confirmations

  • Deposit timestamp and artifact count are captured.
  • Unreviewed-threshold escalation rule is armed.
Output

Artifact enters Intake state with receipt, timer, and canonical run-level acknowledgment in place. If threshold is breached later, Scrum Master notification is triggered.

2Step 2: PO Intake Review

At the next governance session, the Product Owner opens the intake folder and reviews recommendation identity, task-card identifier, source run context, and roadmap fit. Each item receives an explicit decision: admit to the review folder/state, hold in intake with a recorded rationale, or reject with a recorded reason. Passive neglect is not a valid state.

Procedural steps
  1. Run the Step 2 Codex acknowledgment pass after the Step 1 dry-run result has been reviewed and accepted.
  2. Confirm that the triage acknowledgment was appended to governance-records/intake-triage/INTAKE_TRIAGE_LOG.md.
  3. Re-run python3 scripts/run_intake_triage.py and confirm the surfaced artifacts now show Awaiting intake decision instead of New to PO review.
  4. Open one draft artifact from governance-states/01_intake/ and review its recommendation identity, task-card identifier, source run context, and roadmap fit.
  5. Choose exactly one outcome for that artifact: Hold, Admit to review, or Reject.
  6. Record that decision in governance-records/governance-log/GOVERNANCE_LOG.md.
  7. If the decision is Admit to review, move the file into governance-states/02_review/.
  8. If the decision is Hold, leave the file in governance-states/01_intake/ and optionally add a companion _hold.md note beside it.
  9. If the decision is Reject, record the rejection in the governance log and do not leave the outcome implied or undocumented.
  10. Repeat the same sequence for each remaining artifact until every surfaced item has an explicit recorded outcome.
Decision gate
  • Admit: first record `Decision: Admit to review` in governance-records/governance-log/GOVERNANCE_LOG.md, then physically move the draft artifact file from governance-states/01_intake/ into governance-states/02_review/.
  • Hold: record `Decision: Hold`, the rationale, and `Next state: governance-states/01_intake/` in governance-records/governance-log/GOVERNANCE_LOG.md, then leave the draft artifact file in governance-states/01_intake/.
  • Reject: record the rejection in governance-records/governance-log/GOVERNANCE_LOG.md; do not leave rejection as an implied unstated outcome.
Required record

The canonical hold note location is governance-records/governance-log/GOVERNANCE_LOG.md. If a local reminder is helpful, add an optional companion note beside the draft artifact named <draft-artifact-filename>_hold.md, but do not treat that companion file as the system of record.

3Step 3: Review And Staging

The Product Owner evaluates admitted candidates for publication timing, backlog fit, and duplication risk against existing PBIs. Candidates that pass move to Approved state. Rejected candidates are explicitly closed with reason notes rather than disappearing silently.

Approved state only Explicit close reason required
Procedural steps
  1. Open one artifact from governance-states/02_review/.
  2. Review the candidate for publication timing, backlog fit, and duplication risk against existing PBIs.
  3. Decide whether the item should move forward now, remain under review, or be explicitly rejected.
  4. If the item is not ready to move forward, keep it in review with an explicit recorded reason rather than leaving the status implied.
  5. If the item is rejected, record the reason explicitly in the governance log.
  6. If the item passes review and should move forward, move it into governance-states/03_approved/.
  7. Repeat the same sequence for each remaining artifact in governance-states/02_review/.
Checks performed
  • Publish now or defer.
  • Backlog fit and ordering relevance.
  • Collision or duplication risk with existing PBIs.
State rule

Only items in Approved state are eligible for publication or any future MCP-assisted loading path. Intake and Review states are not publishable.

4Step 4: Publication Readiness Check

Publication readiness is an explicit named gate, not an implied judgment. The item must pass the checklist before any create or update action occurs in ClickUp.

Procedural steps
  1. Open one artifact that is already in governance-states/03_approved/.
  2. Decide whether the item should be published now or deferred.
  3. Check that the item does not duplicate or collide with an existing ClickUp PBI.
  4. Identify the exact ClickUp destination backlog, list, and starting status before any publication action is taken.
  5. Decide whether the item should be marked backlog-only or as a later sprint candidate at publication time.
  6. Confirm the draft is clear enough to publish without heavier pre-publication elaboration.
  7. If any readiness check fails, stop publication and continue to Step 5 for pre-publication refinement.
  8. If every readiness check passes, continue to Step 6 for publication.
What must be confirmed before publication
  • The item should be published now.
  • It is not a duplicate or collision with an existing PBI.
  • The correct ClickUp destination backlog, list, and status are identified.
  • The item is backlog-only or a candidate for later sprint selection.
  • The draft is clear enough for Product Owner publication without heavier pre-publication elaboration.
5Step 5: Pre-Publication PBI Refinement If Needed

This step is triggered only when the readiness check exposes gaps that must be resolved before publication. Manual Product Owner refinement is the default. SME, end users, Scrum Team, and LLM assistance may be used to improve title, framing, scope, acceptance structure, constraints, dependencies, and clarity, but the work must remain lean and accountability remains with the Product Owner.

Allowed refinement
  • Clarify problem framing and scope.
  • Tighten acceptance structure and constraints.
  • Surface known dependencies and open questions.
Exit condition

When the gap is resolved, return to the Step 4 readiness gate and reconfirm passage before moving to publication. Refinement is not a substitute for the gate.

6Step 6: Publish Or Create PBI

Publication creates the real PBI in ClickUp for the first time. The default path is manual Product Owner publication. A future MCP-assisted path may load only from Approved state and still requires Product Owner confirmation before commit.

Default path

Manual PO publication: the Product Owner creates or updates the correct ClickUp item directly after destination confirmation and duplicate check.

Optional future path

MCP-assisted publication: an agent may assist from Approved state only, but never without Product Owner confirmation before commit and never as the default path.

One-to-one mapping rule

One approved draft artifact maps to one published PBI. Multiple approved drafts must not be silently collapsed into one ClickUp item.

Destination confirmed Duplicate checked Status set to Backlog-Active
7Step 7: Record Publication Result

Publication is not complete until the result is recorded immediately in the canonical governance log. This log is the single place to reopen or verify publication status and the audit trail connecting the source draft artifact to the live PBI.

Procedural steps
  1. Complete the publication or creation action in ClickUp.
  2. Capture the ClickUp URL or ID before moving on to another task.
  3. Open governance-records/governance-log/GOVERNANCE_LOG.md.
  4. Add a publication record for the newly created PBI.
  5. Record the source draft artifact path, destination list and status, publication date, publication owner, and sprint candidacy.
  6. Include any refinement notes or decision rationale that should remain part of the durable audit trail.
  7. Save the governance log entry before leaving the publication workflow.
What must be recorded after publication
  • ClickUp URL or ID.
  • Destination list and status used at publication time.
  • Linked source draft artifact file path.
  • Publication timestamp and publication owner.
  • Backlog-only or sprint-candidate flag.
  • Any refinement note or decision rationale worth preserving.
8Step 8: Backlog Stewardship

After publication, the PBI remains in Backlog-Active while the Product Owner performs ongoing stewardship. This includes stakeholder collaboration, acceptance criteria maturation, dependency tracking, ordering and relative priority maintenance, and readiness updates in ClickUp. LLM assistance may help improve quality, but Product Owner accountability does not transfer.

Stewardship activities
  • Collaborate with SME, end users, and the dev team.
  • Develop and mature acceptance criteria.
  • Track dependencies, constraints, and backlog priority.
Log discipline

Significant stewardship decisions or status changes are added to the governance log so readiness history is not lost inside informal comments alone.

9Step 9: Sprint-Readiness Confirmation And Handoff

Before the item is eligible for Sprint Kanban selection, the Product Owner explicitly confirms sprint-readiness. Once the gate passes, the PBI is set to Sprint-Eligible. Sprint Kanban later selects from that set as a Product Development Step 1 activity. When selected, the status becomes Sprint-Selected and governance closes after recording selection timestamp and sprint reference.

Status updated to Sprint-Eligible Selection happens downstream Governance closes on Sprint-Selected
Procedural steps
  1. Review the published PBI and confirm it is ready for Sprint Kanban consideration.
  2. Decide explicitly whether the item is sprint-ready now or should remain under backlog stewardship.
  3. If it is sprint-ready, update the ClickUp status to Sprint-Eligible on the live PBI referenced by the publication record in governance-records/governance-log/GOVERNANCE_LOG.md.
  4. Leave sprint commitment and actual selection to the downstream Sprint Kanban process.
  5. When Sprint Kanban later selects the item, update the same ClickUp PBI status to Sprint-Selected and record the selection timestamp and sprint reference in governance-records/governance-log/GOVERNANCE_LOG.md so governance can close cleanly.
Boundary rule

This workflow does not perform sprint commitment, velocity planning, or delivery scheduling. It stops at the prioritized Product Backlog. The PO will work with the Sprintteam to decide on a Sprint goal to pull PBI not push them in from the Product Gevernance Workflow side.
