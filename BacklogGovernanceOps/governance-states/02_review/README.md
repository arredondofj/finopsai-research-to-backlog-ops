# Review

## Purpose

This folder holds draft artifacts that the Product Owner has explicitly admitted from `../01_intake/` for active review. Admission is a deliberate PO decision: items here are being considered for publication but have not yet been approved.

## What belongs here

- Draft artifacts moved from `../01_intake/` by the PO.
- Items under active review for publication timing, backlog fit, and duplication risk.

## PO review actions

- Review each candidate against the publication readiness criteria in `docs/BACKLOG_GOVERNANCE_USAGE.md`.
- Check for duplication or collision with existing ClickUp PBIs before approving.
- Move approved candidates to `../03_approved/`.
- Do not silently discard rejected candidates; close them with a reason note appended to the file or recorded in `../../governance-records/governance-log/GOVERNANCE_LOG.md`.
- Hold items needing more information in this folder with a status note; do not return them to `../01_intake/`.

## What does not belong here

- Items not explicitly admitted by the PO; those stay in `../01_intake/`.
- Items already approved for publication; move those to `../03_approved/`.

## Duplication check reminder

Before approving any item, confirm it does not duplicate or collide with an existing published PBI in ClickUp. Catching a duplicate here is far less costly than after publication, especially because one approved draft artifact must map to exactly one published PBI.
